Helm's Gate is a fortress that acts as bridge between the Mundane Plane and the Plane of Hell. At the end of the [[The Mundane Plane/History/Age of Shattering/Age of Shattering\|Age of Shattering]], Helm gave his life to create the gate, sacrificing himself to forge a permanent barrier between the two planes. His divine essence became bound to the gate itself, sealing of the passage so nothing could cross through it. Ever since the [[Other Planes of Existence/The Seven Hells/Helm/The Helm Guard\|The Helm Guard]] have watched over it. 

Ever since, no major Hell incursion has occurred. However cunning creatures from Hell have found ways to avoid the Gate and cross into the Mundane Plane, these events are isolated and rare.
