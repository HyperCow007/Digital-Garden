The Helm Guard are an ancient order of Champions sworn to protect the Mundane Plane from Hell. Blessed with unnaturally long lives, they have stood watch over [[Other Planes of Existence/The Seven Hells/Helm/Helm's Gate\|Helm's Gate]] since the end of the Age of Shattering, fighting within Hell itself so that its armies can never reach the mortal world. They have maintained the same oath:

*Where Hell reaches, I shall stand.*
*Where the Gate falters, I shall hold.*
*Where darkness comes, I shall be the shield.* 

*My life is Helm's.*
*My blood is the Gate's.*
*My soul shall be Hell's prison.*

*So Long as I draw breath, hell shall not pass.* 

*By Helm's sacrifice, I stand.* 
*By Helm's command, I endure.*
*By my oath, I shall hold.*
