# Lore

Helm was the God of guardianship, vigilance and duty, the one who stood watch when other could no longer do so. Unlike many gods, Helm was not remembered from conquest or glory, but for his unwavering commitment to protecting the mortal world. 

At the end of the [[The Mundane Plane/History/Age of Shattering/Age of Shattering\|Age of Shattering]], when the boundaries between planes were broken and Hell had spilled onto the Mundane Plane, Helm made the ultimate sacrifice. he gave his life to forge [[Other Planes of Existence/The Seven Hells/Helm/Helm's Gate\|Helm's Gate]], binding his divine essence into the barrier that separated the two worlds. 

Helm knew the Gate could never stand alone. So, with his final act je entrusted it's defence to mortal champions. [[Other Planes of Existence/The Seven Hells/Helm/The Helm Guard\|The Helm Guard]] became the continuation of his vigil. Though Helm is gone, the guard still speaks of him as though he is watching.
