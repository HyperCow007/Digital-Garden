# Origin and Nature 
The Fey Wilds, the Fey call it The First World, its a scrapped and work in progress world, where natural systems dominate the landscape. A place where reality and the physical and magical laws of the Mundane Plane breakdown.


# Fey 
The Fey wish to converge The First World, into the Mundane Plane. So their sphere of influence increases.

## The Fey Court
The Fey Court is the governing leadership of The First World. It ruled by the 4 Archfeys, who rule parts of the wilderness. They typically act independent the Court existed for them to have official talks with each other. 

Its know as the Great Game, where all the current Archfeys are the same in power, so they play tricks and schemes against each other, to reduce another strength or influence. If one exceeds the rest, the other will team against them.

## Mortality 
The Fey are immoral within the Fey Wilds, if they die they simply return. However if they die outside their realm, they can truly be killed.
