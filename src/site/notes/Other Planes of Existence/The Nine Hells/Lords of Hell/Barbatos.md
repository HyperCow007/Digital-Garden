Barbatos - Lord of [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The Layers/The Ashen March\|The Ashen March]], [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The 9 Layers of Hell\|The 9 Layers of Hell]]
The First Hunter
Duke of the Hunt


Barbatos was the first being to take dominion over Hell's outer wastes, to hunt strong soles who dared to cross through [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/Helm/Helm's Gate\|Helm's Gate]]. When the gates between Hell and the mortal planes opened during the [[The Mundane Plane/History/Age of Shattering/Age of Shattering\|Age of Shattering]], he was said to be the first one out, the one who hunted the ancient hero's and snuffed out strong prey.
