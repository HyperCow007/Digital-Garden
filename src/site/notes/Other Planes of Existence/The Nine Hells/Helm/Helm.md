# Lore

Helm was the God of guardianship, vigilance and duty, the one who stood watch when other could no longer do so. Unlike many gods, Helm was not remembered from conquest or glory, but for his unwavering commitment to protecting the mortal world. 

At the end of the [[The Mundane Plane/History/Age of Shattering/Age of Shattering\|Age of Shattering]], when the boundaries between planes were broken and [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The 9 Layers of Hell\|The 9 Layers of Hell]] had spilled onto the Mundane Plane, Helm made the ultimate sacrifice. he gave his life to forge [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/Helm/Helm's Gate\|Helm's Gate]], binding his divine essence into the barrier that separated the two worlds. 

Helm knew the Gate could never stand alone. So, with his final act je entrusted it's defence to mortal champions. [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/Helm/The Helm Guard\|The Helm Guard]] became the continuation of his vigil. Though Helm is gone, the guard still speaks of him as though he is watching. 

# Divine Profile 
God of  Protection, Duty, Vigilance, Might

Divine Sanctification: Holy

## Edicts 
*What followers must strive to do*

- **Maintain unceasing vigilance**: Always stay alert, anticipate attacks, and ensure your charges are secure from hidden threats. 
- **Defend those placed in your care**: Protect your designated objective, person or location with your life
- **Uphold honour and duty**: Follow your orders to the letter and honour oaths of protection without complaint 
- **Shield the vulnerable**: Protect children, the injured, the young and the weak when they are targeted

## Anathemas 
*What followers must never do*

- **Abandon your post or fail in vigilance**: Falling asleep on watch, abandoning a guard duty, or neglecting to prepare for an enemy assault. 
- **Break an oath of protection or service**: Betraying a client, employer, or cause you swore a vow to uphold. 
- **Allow personal feelings to override duty**: Letting greed, vengeance, or personal bias distract you from your defensive mandate. 
- **Striking an opponent from ambush or stealth**: Attacking targets from hidden shadows, which violates Helm's rigid doctrine of straightforward, honourable defence.
