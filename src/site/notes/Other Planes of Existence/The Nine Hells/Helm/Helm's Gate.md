Helm's Gate is a fortress that acts as bridge between the Mundane Plane and the Plane of [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The 9 Layers of Hell\|The 9 Layers of Hell]]. At the end of the [[The Mundane Plane/History/Age of Shattering/Age of Shattering\|Age of Shattering]], [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/Helm/Helm\|Helm]] gave his life to create the gate, sacrificing himself to forge a permanent barrier between the two planes. His divine essence became bound to the gate itself, sealing of the passage so nothing could cross through it. Ever since the [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/Helm/The Helm Guard\|The Helm Guard]] have watched over it. 

Ever since, no major Hell incursion has occurred. However cunning creatures from [[Other Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The 9 Layers of Hell\|The 9 Layers of Hell]] have found ways to avoid the Gate and cross into the Mundane Plane, these events are isolated and rare.
