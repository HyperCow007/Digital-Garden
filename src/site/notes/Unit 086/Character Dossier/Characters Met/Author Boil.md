Age: Very old, was around during the [[The Mundane Plane/History/Age of Shattering/Age of Shattering\|Age of Shattering]] 
Race: Undead
Husband of *Lady Spider*, lives in the Labyrinth of Old as the floor 1 overseer 

Was a notable human warrior during the Age of Shattering, however his name know is mostly forgotten to the masses. 

After 1 battle we was approach by someone to come work for him, he would gain immortality and could fight to his hearts content. However soon he discovered he was trapped within the Labyrinth to forever oversee it and fight whoever he came across. His wife the *Lady Spider*
also moved with him to the labyrinth to keep him company, and to raise her young.

He did get bored of fighting, hence his passive stance today.
