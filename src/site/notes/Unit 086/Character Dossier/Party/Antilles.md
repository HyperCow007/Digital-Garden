# Lore
**Full Name**: Antilles Urion
**Age**: 34
**Ancestry**: Ratfolk

An infamous smuggler and anti-imperial rebel. He has dedicated his whole life to sneaking things from one place to another. Gold, letters, animals and whatever needed to be discreetly delivered. After accidentally murdering a nobleman's daughter in a robbery gone bad, Antilles handed himself in to the authorities. Knowing he would be turned into a weapon.
