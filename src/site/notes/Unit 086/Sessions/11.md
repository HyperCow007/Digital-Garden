  - A climb through a alpine forest
    - Requires 6 success before 3 fails

    They ambush the the party over a rocky ledge 

  - Discovery
    - The Antilles notices enormous white veins running through the black stone of the mountain. At first glance they appear to be marble. 
    - DC20 Perception  - they curve like they are bones

    - The forest is silent, Too silent. 
    - During the night character hears footsteps circling the camp
    - Nothing there, the tracks begin and end, without ever leaving the campsite
    - Perception checks gave no answers

*Roll D20 at the Start of each day on a 20 (each player), the Dragon appears. If honest to the three old men about who they were meeting it will give them a ride to the top.*
