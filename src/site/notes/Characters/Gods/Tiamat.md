# Lore

Few beings in recorded history command the same level of fear as Tiamat. Tiamat is the master of all chromatic dragons, her will etched into their very soul. 

During [[The Mundane Plane/History/Age of Accord/The Battle of Ashen Night\|The Battle of Ashen Night]], Tiamat descended upon the world in full fury, raised a army the world hasn't seen since the [[The Mundane Plane/History/Age of Shattering/Age of Shattering\|Age of Shattering]]. Even the summoned heroes being drawn from another world were barley enough to force her to retreat. Victory was never truly achieved, only borrowed time.

Now she resides with [[The Mundane Plane/Vaeloris/The Northern Reach/Emberfall\|Emberfall]], a scorched and broken region twisted by her presence. The land itself reflects her lingering power, and creatures warped into draconic abominations.

Current intel suggest, that she is still recovering from her wounds, left on her by the hero William.

# Divine Profile 
Dragon God of Destruction, Tyranny, Greed and Conquest 

Divine Sanctification: unholy

## Edicts 
*What followers must strive to do*

- **Amass wealth and power**: Accumulate vast riches, artifacts and personal hoards to honour the Dragon Queen 
- **Enforce strict hierarchy**: Rule over inferiors with an iron fist, demanding absolute obedience from subordinates 
- **Avenge all slights**: Retaliate ruthlessly against any creature that insults, defines or acts against you or Tiamat.
- **Assert Tiamat's dominance**: Elevate the status of Tiamat and subvert, enslave, or destroy those who oppose her.


## Anathemas 
*What followers must never do*

- **Show genuine mercy or pity**: Allowing a broken defeated foe to go free without demanding total submission, enslavement, or tribute.
- **Part with wealth willingly**: Giving away treasure, paying unfair taxes, or offering charity with a clear manipulative strategic advantage. 
- **Accept parity with equals**: Allowing someone of equal standing to command you without planning their eventual downfall or subordination.
- **Suffer a slight from a follower of Bahamut**: Letting ant agent, champion or dragon of Bahamut escape your wrath if its within your power to destroy them.
