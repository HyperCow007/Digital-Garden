# Basic Synopsis 

To the humans its a age of beginning, within elven records its regarded as a near extinction event. 

The conflicted persisted for four millennia. During this time, the mortal races alongside the remaining active deities, engaged in open warfare against entities originating from Hell and multiple outer planes. To restore the balance lost when the prime creator died.

Documented consequences
- The near extinction of long lived races and other non-human races
- Loss of almost all the Gods
- Loss of the Prime creator 

Analysis of the conflict indicates that primary disadvantaged faced by the elder race was not military power but reproductive limitations. 

By the latter stages of the age, multiple sources indicate that several races approached extinction or were lost forever.
