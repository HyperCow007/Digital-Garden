This regions is the largest in [[The Mundane Plane/Continent - Vaeloris/Vaeloris/The Southern Dunes/The Southern Dunes\|The Southern Dunes]], and is mostly uninhabited due to the extreme heat conditions. It's the harshest stretch of land on Vaeloris. 

Remanets of the old Southern Dune tribes live within it, groups of people who haven't conformed to the [[The Mundane Plane/Continent - Vaeloris/Factions - Vaeloris/Federated Holy Empire/Federated Holy Empire\|Federated Holy Empire]]. These people are known as the Vidrohi (*Vid-draw-he*).
