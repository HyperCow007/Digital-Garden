The Military is a  diverse collection of Holy Knight Orders. As each region throughout the realm has its own order who are trained to fight within that climate or terrain. From this standard gear, battle tactics, and discipline differ from regiment to regiment. 

A Holy Knight Order is typically made up of a handful of companies, each companies may consist of 2000 to 3000 troops. This is further broken down for organisation and logistics. Each regiment is typically named after the noble family associated with that region. The highest ranking officer of the Military is referred to as the Grandmaster. The Current Grand Master is [[The Mundane Plane/Continent - Vaeloris/Factions - Vaeloris/Federated Holy Empire/Notable Characters (FHE)/Ligar Heospan\|Ligar Heospan]]. 

[[The Mundane Plane/Continent - Vaeloris/Factions - Vaeloris/Federated Holy Empire/The Church of Bahamut/The Faith\|The Faith]] and The Military are known to disagree with each other, over a many difference of opinions and the way they handle affairs. The military tends to take a pragmatic view, treating lost artifacts and ancient technology as resources to be studied rather than revered.  

The military also control the deploys the [[The Mundane Plane/Continent - Vaeloris/Factions - Vaeloris/Federated Holy Empire/The Imperial Military/The Legend Units\|The Legend Units]] putting them out on the frontline or sending them on missions to fatal for regular troops. 

Currently there are 11 Holy Knight Orders, in operation, one for each of the regions.
