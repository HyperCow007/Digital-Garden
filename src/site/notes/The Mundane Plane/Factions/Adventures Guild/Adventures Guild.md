The Adventures Guild for many years have been responsible for exploration and monster hunting throughout the world some would grow to become hero's of renown. However after the invention of [[The Mundane Plane/Continent - Vaeloris/Factions - Vaeloris/Federated Holy Empire/The Imperial Military/The Legend Units\|The Legend Units]], the empire can send those units out on these dangerous mission, at the fraction of the cost. Due to this adventures have a even greater distain for the [[The Mundane Plane/Continent - Vaeloris/Factions - Vaeloris/Federated Holy Empire/The Imperial Military/The Legend Units\|The Legend Units]], as many guilds and companies within it shut down, or moved on to different types of business. 

Adventures nowadays still do what they used to, but to a lesser degree, and more help local towns and station themselves in settlements to deal with minor threats and the odd monster hunt or exploration mission.

Due to this change in their job climate, the rise of companies within the guild, have shifted course many shut down. But a few or moved on to different types of business, or tried to keep their old titles and jobs relevant in this new age. 

Mercenary companies, generally originated from the adventure groups who decided to expand their sphere of influence.

The Adventures Guild is known for its flexibly of jobs on offer, and who works under the Guild.
