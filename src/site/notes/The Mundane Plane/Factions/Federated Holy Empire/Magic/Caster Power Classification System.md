The [[The Mundane Plane/Factions/Federated Holy Empire/Federated Holy Empire\|Federated Holy Empire]], has a classification system to determine a casters power level. This is typically based on what rank spell they are able to cast. 

To go up in rank, a participant  requires another caster of a higher rank to act as witness to determine if the participant is  cable of casting to that level. 

# Caster Classes

## Apprentice
- 1st and 2nd level spells and cantrips 
- Cannot teach independently 
- Limited access to magical libraries 

## Intermediate
- 3rd and 4th level spells
- Can join military mage corps 
- Eligible for guild membership 
- May teach apprentice class mages 

## Master 
- 5th and 6th level spells
- Know as the great plateau for casters power level, getting over this level is incredibly difficult 
- May supervise magical research
- Eligible to hold governmental positions 

## Saint 
- 7th level spells
- Can lead magical expeditions 
- Can teach lower classes 

## King 
- 8th level spells 
- Considered s strategic military resource 
- May authorise research into restricted magical theory 


## Emperor
- 9th level spells
- Legendary status 
- Considered as some of the strongest on the plane. 

## God 
- 10th level spells 
- Typically the Empire has rewrote laws specifically around their abelites 
- Considered as some of the strongest across multiple planes
