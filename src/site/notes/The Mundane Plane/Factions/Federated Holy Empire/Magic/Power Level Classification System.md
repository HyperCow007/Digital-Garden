The [[The Mundane Plane/Factions/Federated Holy Empire/Federated Holy Empire\|Federated Holy Empire]], has a classification system to determine power level. This is typically based on training, merit, and experience. 

To go up in rank, a participant requires another person of a higher rank to act as witness to determine if the participant is cable reaching the next stage. This is typically conducted by the [[The Mundane Plane/Factions/Organisations/Adventures Guild\|Adventures Guild]]. 

# Power Class

## Apprentice
Casters/Martials
- Cannot teach independently 
- Limited access to magical libraries 

## Intermediate
Casters/Martials
- Can join military mage corps 
- May teach apprentice class mages 
- Can join military corps 
- May teach apprentice class  

## Master 
Casters/Martials
- Know as the great plateau for casters power level, getting over this level is incredibly difficult 
- May supervise magical research
- Eligible to hold governmental positions 

## Saint 
Casters/Martials
- Can lead magical expeditions 
- Can teach lower classes 

## King 
Casters/Martials
- Considered s strategic military resource 
- May authorise research into restricted theory 


## Emperor
Casters/Martials
- Legendary status 
- Considered as some of the strongest on the plane.

## God 
Casters/Martials
- Typically the Empire has rewrote laws specifically around their abelites 
- Considered as some of the strongest across multiple planes
- Typically given a title for reaching this rank
