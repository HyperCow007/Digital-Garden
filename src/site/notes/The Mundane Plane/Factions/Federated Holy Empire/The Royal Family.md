The Royal family is a line chosen by Bahamut himself, in the eyes of [[The Mundane Plane/Factions/Federated Holy Empire/The Faith\|The Faith]] they are a symbol that the path the mortals are on is the right path, the Faith act as advisors to the family. The highest ranks of the [[The Mundane Plane/Factions/Federated Holy Empire/The Imperial Military\|The Imperial Military]] report directly to the Emperor himself.
